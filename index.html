The problem

Four out of five rural Nigerian households still cook with firewood and charcoal.

The cost is invisible but devastating — indoor air pollution, deforestation, hours lost gathering fuel, and a daily health burden falling hardest on women and children. Clean cooking gas exists, but the supply chain stops at the cities.
